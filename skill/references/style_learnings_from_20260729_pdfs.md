# Style Learnings From The Two Reference PDFs

Use these lessons for future US market daily recaps. Do not copy the source reports; adapt the analytical habits.

## 1. GS Chart Of The Day: Factor/Chartbook Page

What to learn:

- Lead with one sharp thesis, not a generic market summary.
- Explain the market beneath the index: SPX can be near highs while AI/momentum factors are in severe drawdown.
- Always ask whether index stability hides factor pain, gross-downs, short-covering, or sector-pair reversal.
- Add factor lenses when available: momentum baskets, TMT momentum, AI baskets, SPX ex AI, stock correlation, realized volatility, hedge-fund gross leverage, L/S momentum exposure.
- When momentum is broken but positioning has cleared, separate "capitulation/buy-the-dip setup" from "trend still broken".
- If volatility is elevated, mention whether limited-loss structures, short-dated gamma, or long-delta/short-vol structures are more appropriate than outright exposure.

Reusable chart ideas:

- AI basket vs SPX ex AI.
- Momentum factor YTD or drawdown chart.
- TMT momentum drawdown chart.
- Momentum volatility vs SPX realized volatility.
- Hedge fund gross leverage or L/S exposure.
- SPX average stock correlation.
- Factor monitor heatmap.

## 2. US Equities Color: Trading-Desk Recap

What to learn:

- Start with a precise tape snapshot: SPX, NDX, Dow, R2K, total volume versus YTD average, MOC buy/sell imbalance, VIX, WTI, 10Y, gold, DXY, BTC.
- Identify "wrong-way" days: long legs fall while short/funding legs rise. This matters more than the index move.
- Distinguish real sponsorship from short-covering. A software rally without real sponsorship may simply be hedge-fund gross-down and momentum basket unwinds.
- Treat staples or low-vol defensives rallying after strong EPS as possible reversal of the funding short used to finance TMT/momentum longs.
- Track strategy pain when available: Fundamental L/S, Systematic L/S, Multi-Strat, prime-book long selling, gross exposure reduction.
- Add derivatives color when available: index realized vol, single-stock/factor vol, skew, implied move, short-dated call/put flows, sector gamma chase.
- Include post-bell items separately and never use them to explain the cash-session tape unless clearly marked.

Reusable sections:

- "Wrong-way price action": what was long, what was short, and which side caused pain.
- "Real sponsorship or short-covering?": whether the move has fresh fundamental buyers.
- "Prime/positioning": long selling, short covering, gross-downs, factor exposure.
- "Derivatives/flow": vol, skew, gamma, notable options prints.
- "Post-bell watch": earnings and prints relevant to the next session.

## 3. Word Report Layout To Emulate

Future reports should be delivered as Word documents with:

- A first-page executive tape snapshot.
- A "one-sentence thesis" near the top.
- Compact tables for cross-asset, sector, and biggest movers.
- Inline charts/screenshots next to the section that uses them, not only at the end.
- Captions and source lines under every chart.
- A chart/factor appendix when there are more than 3 charts.
- Clear separation between cash-session facts, post-bell facts, and inference.

## 4. Analytical Red Flags To Add

Always check whether the day contains:

- Index strength hiding factor damage.
- AI down while ex-AI is at or near highs.
- Momentum longs being sold and shorts/funding legs squeezing.
- Defensive staples/healthcare/low-vol stocks replacing AI as the active buyer focus.
- Software/internet rally caused by short-covering rather than fundamental sponsorship.
- Memory/semis moving because of China substitution, long-term supply agreements, vendor financing, export controls, or equipment localization.
